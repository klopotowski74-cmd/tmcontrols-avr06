1 Safety first

Isolate the machine before touching the board. Switch off and lock out the control cabinet supply before connecting or disconnecting any terminal plug, the D-sub cable or the service display.
  • Work only with dry hands and on a de-energised board. Handle the board by its edges.
  • The resistors and voltage regulators near the power terminals run warm in normal operation. Do not touch them right after power-off.
  • Plug the terminal blocks back in exactly as they were. Take a photo before you unplug anything.
  • This guide is for qualified maintenance staff. Do not modify components on the board.

Before installing a replacement board

Set JP1 and JP2 exactly as on the old board. JP1 (marked 2^0 ->) and JP2 (marked 2^1 ->) are the two small slide switches next to the ISP header. They set the Modbus address of the board. Machines on the same line can use different settings, so always copy the positions from the board you are replacing. Take a photo of the old board first.

2 Overview

The AVR06 is a 24 V DC machine controller with opto-isolated inputs, transistor outputs, analogue outputs and a Modbus serial port. The T&M Controls board is a drop-in replacement for the original WHERRY board: same size, same mounting holes, same terminal positions and the same original firmware.

3 Power supply

The board is powered from 24 V DC on the bottom terminal blocks. All terminals marked 24V are connected together on the board, and so are all terminals marked GND. One 24 V wire and one GND wire are enough to power the whole board.

Terminal block24VGND
J10pins 3 - 6pins 1 - 2
J11pins 1 - 2pins 3 - 6
Check polarity. The board has no reverse-polarity protection. Always compare with the 24V / GND print next to the terminals before switching on.

Bench test

  1. Set a lab supply to 24.0 V with a current limit of 0.30 A.
  2. Connect 24V and GND on J11.
  3. The red POWER VCC +5V LED lights. The supply should stay in constant-voltage mode (no current limiting).

5 Service display

The board has a 16-pin socket DIS2 for a 2-line character display. On T&M Premium boards the display is fitted as standard. On boards without a display, a service display can be plugged in while the board is switched off.

  • Press the SELECT button next to the display to step through the screens.
  • If the text is not visible or only dark blocks appear, adjust contrast with the small blue trimmer RV1 using a small screwdriver.
  • The display is for information only. Nothing can be changed or damaged by browsing the screens.

7 Heartbeat output Q5

While the controller is running, output Q5 on J7 switches on and off continuously. On the CD4094_2 screen this is the blinking fifth digit.

Q5 blinking = the controller is alive and the program is running.
Q5 not blinking = the program has stopped. Power-cycle the machine. If it does not recover, contact support.

8 Troubleshooting

POWER LED is off
  • Measure the voltage between a 24V and a GND terminal: it should be 22 - 28 V DC.
  • Check that the terminal plugs are fully seated and the screws are tight.
  • If 24 V is present on the terminals and the LED stays off, the board needs repair.
Display is blank or shows only dark blocks
  • Turn the contrast trimmer RV1 slowly through its whole range.
  • Check that the display is seated straight in the DIS2 socket (switch off first).
  • Dark blocks on the top line that never change mean the program did not start: see "Heartbeat".
An input does not react
  • Activate the sensor or switch and watch the matching input LED.
  • LED off: check the field wiring and the 24 V supply of the sensor.
  • LED on, but the bit on the LS166 screen stays 1: the board needs repair.
An output does not switch
  • Check on the CD4094 screen whether the program is switching the output on (1).
  • Bit is 1 but the device does not work: check the device, its fuse and wiring. The output switches to 0 V, so the device must be fed from +24 V.
  • Bit stays 0: the machine sequence is not requesting this output. Check the machine first.
No Modbus communication
  • Check that the D-sub cable is plugged into the male 9-pin D-sub (SERIAL / X7 on the original board), not into the female one, and both screws are tight.
  • Check that the slide switches JP1 (2^0 ->) and JP2 (2^1 ->) are in the same positions as on the original board from this machine.

Enclosure seal (warranty)

The enclosure of every board is closed with a numbered warranty seal. The number on this seal is the board number. The same number is printed on the enclosure label, on the label underneath the board and on the delivery note.

Do not remove or break the enclosure seal. The terminals, both D-sub connectors, the display and the SELECT button can be used without opening the sealed part of the enclosure. A removed or broken enclosure seal voids the warranty.

Box seal (transport)

The box is closed with a separate tamper-evident seal. It only shows that the box has not been opened since it left T&M Controls. Its number is recorded on the delivery note, but it is not the board number.

Opening the box to install the board is expected and does not affect your support. If the box seal is already broken on delivery, please contact us before installing the board.

Board label

The same board number, together with a QR code linking to this guide, is printed on a permanent label on the underside of the board. It stays with the board inside the enclosure, so the number and this guide are always available, even if the box and the documents have been lost.

You do not need this label in normal use: the board number is also on the enclosure seal and on the enclosure label. The label underneath the board is inside the sealed enclosure and is intended for repair and identification by T&M Controls.
